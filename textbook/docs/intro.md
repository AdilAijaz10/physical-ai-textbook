# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Textbook

## Welcome to the Future of Robotics Education

This comprehensive textbook provides a complete educational resource for learning Physical AI and Humanoid Robotics. Through 13 weeks of structured content, you will explore the cutting-edge intersection of robotics, artificial intelligence, and humanoid systems.

### Course Structure

The textbook is organized into 4 main modules:

1. **Module 1 (Weeks 3-5): The Robotic Nervous System (ROS 2)** - Learn ROS 2 architecture, nodes, topics, services, Python integration with rclpy, and URDF for humanoid robots.

2. **Module 2 (Weeks 6-7): The Digital Twin (Gazebo & Unity)** - Explore physics simulation in Gazebo, high-fidelity rendering in Unity, and sensor simulation (LiDAR, cameras, IMUs).

3. **Module 3 (Weeks 8-10): The AI-Robot Brain (NVIDIA Isaac)** - Master Isaac Sim for photorealistic simulation, Isaac ROS for VSLAM and navigation, and Nav2 for bipedal movement.

4. **Module 4 (Weeks 11-13): Vision-Language-Action (VLA)** - Implement voice commands with OpenAI Whisper, LLM cognitive planning, and complete a capstone project: Autonomous Humanoid.

### Learning Approach

This textbook combines theoretical concepts with practical implementation. Each week includes:
- Detailed explanations of core concepts
- Code examples with step-by-step instructions
- Practical exercises and challenges
- Diagrams and visual aids
- Links to additional resources
